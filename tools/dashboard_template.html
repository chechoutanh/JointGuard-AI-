Simulated robot: joint health

Scenario . Every joint is swept once a day; this page shows what those sweeps say.

Simulation only. The wear of every joint is set by the simulation and read back from its own friction sweeps. Nothing on this page was measured on a robot.

What this page does not show. It has no forecast of the service day: that part is not built. It says nothing about a real robot.

The health index is (Coulomb friction / the joint's own first week - 1) / 1.5. It uses the simulation's own wear gain and temperature coefficient, which a real system would have to estimate. Made by python -m tools.dashboard.
